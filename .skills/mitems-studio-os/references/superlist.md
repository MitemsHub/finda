# Reference Teardown: Superlist

> **Essence:** Superlist rejects sterile-minimal productivity aesthetics in favor of **bold color, characterful illustration, and playful motion**. It's a bet that personality and delight differentiate in a crowded, boring category. High energy, high craft.
> **Source:** https://superlist.com · analyzed 2026-07-08 · confidence: inferred (well-established public design language)

> ⚖️ **Copying-line status:** PASS — principles only. Superlist's brand colors as-such, illustrations, wordmark, and copy are NOT reproduced.

## 1. Design Philosophy
**Differentiate by daring.** In a market of austere gray productivity tools, Superlist chooses maximal personality — saturated palettes, expressive type, animated characters/scenes. The philosophy: a to-do app is used daily, so make it *joyful*. Craft keeps the boldness from becoming chaos.

## 2. Typography Breakdown
- **Family (inferred):** a bold, characterful display face for headlines paired with a clean body sans.
- **Hierarchy:** big, punchy, high-contrast display type as a lead actor; type is part of the personality, not just information.

| Role | Size (approx) | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | ~56–96px | 700+ | expressive, bold |
| Sub | ~20–24px | 400–500 | friendly |
| Body | ~16px | 400 | clean |

## 3. Color Palette
- **Saturated, multi-color, high-energy** — an intentional exception to one-accent restraint, justified by "personality as positioning." Bold color blocks; playful combinations. Contrast must be carefully managed at this saturation.

## 4. Spacing Scale
- Dynamic; large expressive sections; color blocks and illustration fill space with energy rather than austere whitespace.

## 5. Grid System
- Flexible, editorial, sometimes asymmetric/overlapping compositions for energy (intentional grid-breaks). Capped content within lively sections.

## 6. Motion Language
- **Rich, characterful motion** — animated scenes, playful transitions, scroll interactions. Motion is a core brand asset here (opposite pole from Linear's restraint). Reduced-motion handling is essential.

## 7. Interaction Patterns
- Playful hovers, animated demos of the product's personality, expressive CTAs. The brand's energy is carried through interaction, not just visuals.

## 8. UX Principles
- **Personality as differentiation** (like Avnac, at higher intensity) → [`02`](../02-PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md), [`03`](../03-BRAND_STRATEGY.md).
- **Delight in a boring category** as a wedge.
- **Craft tames boldness** — high production value prevents chaos.

## 9. Reusable Ideas (as principles)
- **Bold color/motion can be a moat in a sterile category** — differentiate by daring (with discipline) → [`02`](../02-PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md), [`03`](../03-BRAND_STRATEGY.md).
- **Type as a lead character**, not just information → [`07`](../07-TYPOGRAPHY_SYSTEM.md).
- **Motion as a brand asset** (when the brand warrants it) → [`23`](../23-MOTION_SYSTEM.md), [`24`](../24-MICRO_INTERACTIONS.md).
- **Intentional asymmetry/grid-breaks** for energy → [`10`](../10-GRID_SYSTEM.md).

## 10. Things to Avoid
- Saturated multi-color easily **fails contrast** — rigorously verify AA on all text/UI ([`06`](../06-COLOR_SYSTEM.md), [`22`](../22-ACCESSIBILITY.md)).
- Rich motion risks performance + vestibular harm — must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and stay performant ([`23`](../23-MOTION_SYSTEM.md), [`35`](../35-PERFORMANCE.md)).
- Boldness without craft becomes noise; personality must still be *usable* ([`04`](../04-DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY.md) P11).

## 11. How to Recreate This Style Without Copying
1. If your category is sterile, **choose personality** as a wedge — derive a bold palette/motion language from *your* brand story (not Superlist's).
2. Make **type a character**; use expressive display with a clean body.
3. Use **rich motion as a brand asset** — but reduced-motion-safe and performant.
4. **Verify contrast** obsessively at high saturation.
5. Let **craft discipline** keep boldness from becoming chaos.
6. Diverge with your own palette, illustration, and motion character.
