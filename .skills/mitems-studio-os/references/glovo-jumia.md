# Reference Teardown: Glovo & Jumia (comparative)

> **Essence:** Two African delivery/commerce giants, deliberately compared. **Glovo** — vivid **yellow (`~#FFC244`) + green (`~#00A082`)** with a well-considered, delightful, polished app. **Jumia** — **orange + white**, brand-recognizable but widely critiqued as utilitarian/"barest minimum." The lesson isn't which brand is bigger; it's what *design investment* buys in a competitive market.
> **Source:** glovoapp.com / jumia.com.ng · analyzed 2026-07-08 · confidence: inferred (public design-analysis corroboration)

> ⚖️ **Copying-line status:** PASS — principles + a comparative lesson. No logos/assets/copy reproduced; hex recorded for study.

## 1. Design Philosophy
**Glovo:** delight and polish as competitive edge — a playful, high-contrast yellow/green identity applied with care (thoughtful color play, smooth UX) so the app is one "users fall in love with." **Jumia:** reach and function first — a recognizable orange brand but minimal design craft, prioritizing catalog breadth/operations over experience polish. Together they illustrate the studio's thesis: in crowded markets, **craft is a differentiator** (Art. VII), and "barest minimum" is a strategic risk.

## 2. Typography Breakdown
- **Glovo:** friendly, rounded brand type; clear hierarchy; considered.
- **Jumia:** largely system/utilitarian type; functional, dense catalog hierarchy (title → price → description). Neither is wrong for its goal, but the *care gap* is visible.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Glovo:** yellow `~#FFC244` + green `~#00A082` — a warm/energetic + fresh/trust pairing; high-contrast and distinctive (appetite + freshness psychology).
- **Jumia:** orange (`~#F68B1E`-family) + white — appetite/energy, strong recognition, but limited systematized depth.
- **Lesson:** both use warm/appetite hues (category convention), but Glovo *systematizes and applies with craft*; Jumia does the minimum.

## 4. Spacing & Grid
- **Glovo:** considered spacing, polished cards, smooth flows.
- **Jumia:** dense catalog grids optimized for breadth/scanning many SKUs (marketplace pattern) — function over finesse.

## 5–7. Motion / Interaction / Density
- **Glovo:** smoother transitions, delight moments, cohesive UX.
- **Jumia:** utilitarian; heavy promo banners, dense listings, aggressive merchandising — effective for volume, weaker on delight.

## 8. UX Principles (the comparative takeaways)
- **Craft is a moat** in crowded consumer categories — Glovo's polish earns affection; Jumia's minimalism earns critique despite scale ([`00`](../00-CONSTITUTION.md) Art. VII, [`02`](../02-PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md)).
- **Color psychology + contrast, applied with care**, builds a lovable brand ([`06`](../06-COLOR_SYSTEM.md), [`05`](../05-VISUAL_PSYCHOLOGY.md)).
- **"Barest minimum" is a strategic vulnerability** — functional-but-bland invites a better-designed competitor ([`04`](../04-DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY.md)).
- **Marketplace density** (Jumia) is legitimate for breadth but must still respect hierarchy/clarity ([`27`](../27-INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md)).

## 9. Reusable Ideas (as principles)
- **Invest in craft to differentiate** where competitors do the minimum → Art. VII, [`02`](../02-PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md).
- **A distinctive, high-contrast two-color identity** (applied consistently) is memorable → [`06`](../06-COLOR_SYSTEM.md), [`03`](../03-BRAND_STRATEGY.md).
- **Even dense marketplaces need hierarchy** (title→price→desc) and clarity → [`11`](../11-CARD_DESIGN.md), [`27`](../27-INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Delight/polish drives affection + retention** in consumer apps → [`24`](../24-MICRO_INTERACTIONS.md), [`18`](../18-SAAS_DESIGN.md).

## 10. Things to Avoid
- **Jumia's lesson:** shipping "barest minimum" design leaves you exposed to a more crafted rival — don't mistake scale for safety ([`50`](../50-CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT.md)).
- **Glovo's caution:** high-saturation yellow/green must still meet **contrast** for text/CTAs ([`22`](../22-ACCESSIBILITY.md)).
- Both: delivery ethics — **transparent fees/ETAs, honest promos** (no fake urgency), no hidden charges (Art. III, [`19`](../19-ECOMMERCE_DESIGN.md)).
- Heavy promo banners (Jumia) can overwhelm + hurt performance ([`35`](../35-PERFORMANCE.md)).

## 11. How to Recreate These Lessons Without Copying
1. **Decide your craft investment deliberately** — in a crowded market, polish (Glovo) is a moat; "minimum" (Jumia) is a risk.
2. Build a **distinctive, high-contrast identity** in *your* hues (not yellow/green or orange specifically), applied consistently and accessibly.
3. If a marketplace, respect **card hierarchy + IA** even at high density.
4. Add **delight** (micro-interactions, smooth flows) to earn affection.
5. Keep fees/promos **transparent and honest**.
6. Diverge with your own palette, type, and motion.
