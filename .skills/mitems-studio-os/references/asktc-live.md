# Reference Teardown: ASKTC

> **Essence:** "The best question in the room never gets asked." A live audience-Q&A/polling SaaS whose landing is a model of SaaS clarity: emotional problem hook → hard proof stats (73% never asked; 60s setup) → **dual product mockups** (what the moderator sees vs. what the audience sees) → competitor contrast (vs. Slido) → transparent 3-tier pricing → FAQ → CTA.
> **Source:** https://asktc.live · analyzed 2026-07-08 · confidence: mixed (live homepage capture)

> ⚖️ **Copying-line status:** PASS — principles only. No assets/copy reproduced.

## 1. Design Philosophy
ASKTC leads with a **relatable pain** everyone at an event has felt, then relentlessly builds trust with concrete numbers and *seeing the actual product from both sides*. It's warm, human, and confident — clearly localized too (Lagos Tech Summit, Telegram bot, "local payment options"), a smart contextual signal for its market. The design serves comprehension and conversion over decoration.

## 2. Typography Breakdown
- **Family (inferred):** clean modern sans; strong, friendly display for the hook.
- **Hierarchy:** big empathetic headline ("The best question in the room never gets asked."), rotating audience words (universities/conferences/churches), **oversized stat numbers** (73%, 1 in 8, 60s) as pre-attentive anchors, clear section headers framed as narrative beats ("The problem", "How it works", "Pricing").

## 3. Color Palette
- Restrained with a clear action accent for CTAs ("Create your first event"); functional status color in the live-queue mockups (vote counts, live badges, sentiment bars — always paired with text). Clean, trustworthy, not loud.

## 4. Spacing Scale
- Generous, well-sectioned; each concept (problem, dual views, features, how-it-works, pricing, FAQ) gets a distinct, breathing band — an easy scannable scroll.

## 5. Grid System
- Capped content; **dual-mockup layouts** (moderator screen + audience phone) side by side; 3-column pricing; numbered 3-step "how it works"; card-based feature rows.

## 6. Motion Language
- Rotating audience-type words in the hero; live-updating mockup counters (inferred); gentle reveals. Motion supports the "live/real-time" product story. Reduced-motion handling needed.

## 7. Interaction Patterns
- Dual primary paths done right: "Create your first event" (host) vs. "Join an event" (attendee) — two audiences, clearly separated. Transparent, comparative **pricing tiers** with a "most popular" flag (ethical default). FAQ accordion. Repeated single primary CTA.

## 8. UX Principles
- **Problem-first storytelling** — hook with a felt pain before pitching ([`17`](../17-LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md), [`05`](../05-VISUAL_PSYCHOLOGY.md)).
- **Show both sides of a two-sided product** (moderator + audience) so each persona sees themselves → [`26`](../26-USER_EXPERIENCE.md).
- **Hard, specific stats** as trust anchors → [`16`](../16-DASHBOARD_DESIGN.md), [`17`](../17-LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md).
- **Transparent pricing** with an honest "most popular" nudge → [`05`](../05-VISUAL_PSYCHOLOGY.md) (ethical), [`17`](../17-LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md).
- **Localize trust signals** (Telegram, local payments, regional names) for the target market → [`03`](../03-BRAND_STRATEGY.md).

## 9. Reusable Ideas (as principles)
- **Problem → proof stats → dual product view → pricing → FAQ → CTA** is a highly reusable SaaS landing spine → [`17`](../17-LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md).
- **Dual mockups for two-sided products** let each persona self-identify → [`26`](../26-USER_EXPERIENCE.md).
- **Oversized, specific stats** as pre-attentive trust anchors → [`05`](../05-VISUAL_PSYCHOLOGY.md).
- **Transparent, comparative pricing** with an honest recommended tier → [`02`](../02-PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md), [`05`](../05-VISUAL_PSYCHOLOGY.md).
- **Localized trust cues** as differentiation in a specific market → [`03`](../03-BRAND_STRATEGY.md).

## 10. Things to Avoid
- Stats ("73% of attendees…") must be **real and sourced** — invented statistics are a trust/ethics violation (Art. III, [`17`](../17-LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md) honest proof).
- Dual primary CTAs (host vs. join) work only because the audiences are truly distinct — otherwise it splits focus ([`12`](../12-BUTTON_DESIGN.md)).
- Live-updating mockups shouldn't imply real data if simulated (label clearly).
- Comparative "vs. Slido" framing must be fair, not a strawman ([`05`](../05-VISUAL_PSYCHOLOGY.md)).

## 11. How to Recreate This Style Without Copying
1. Open with a **felt problem** your user recognizes instantly, then back it with **real, sourced stats**.
2. If two-sided, show **both personas' views** side by side so each self-identifies.
3. Use **oversized specific numbers** as trust anchors (verifiable).
4. Provide **transparent, comparative pricing** with an honest recommended tier.
5. Add **localized trust cues** relevant to your actual market.
6. Diverge with your own hook, palette, and product mockups.
