# Reference Teardown: AgriTech (ThriveAgric & Farmcrowdy)

> **Essence:** The African agri-tech archetype — platforms linking **smallholder farmers** with **inputs, financing, and markets**, often with an investment/funding side. Design must bridge a **rural↔digital divide** and earn trust across two very different audiences (farmers on the ground; funders/buyers online). The toolkit: **real farmer photography**, **earthy/green "growth" palettes**, **impact metrics** (farmers empowered, hectares, yields), and **transparency** (where money/produce goes).
> **Source:** thriveagric.com / farmcrowdy.com · analyzed 2026-07-08 · confidence: inferred (public design language of the sector)

> ⚖️ **Copying-line status:** PASS — principles only. No assets/copy/logos reproduced. Studied as the *agriculture archetype*.

## 1. Design Philosophy
Agri-tech sells **trust and impact**. For farmers: approachability, local language/context, low-friction access (often mobile/USSD/agent-assisted). For funders/buyers/partners: **credibility and transparency** — real photos of real farms/farmers (not stock), concrete impact numbers, and clarity on how inputs/financing/returns work. The emotional register is **hopeful, grounded, authentic** — growth and empowerment, not slick fintech gloss.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Earthy + green "growth"** palettes (greens, soil browns, warm naturals) signaling agriculture, freshness, and growth; clean surfaces; real photography supplies warmth/authenticity. (Nature/growth color psychology, [`06`](../06-COLOR_SYSTEM.md).)

## 4. Spacing & Grid
- Comfortable, photo-forward; capped content; impact-metric bands; **dual-audience sections** (farmer path vs. funder/partner path); card grids for programs/produce.

## 5. Motion Language
- Gentle, functional; photo/impact reveals. Authenticity + clarity over spectacle. Reduced-motion aware.

## 6. Interaction Patterns
- **Dual-audience routing** (farmers onboard/access inputs vs. investors/partners engage); impact dashboards/reports; program/produce cards; often **low-bandwidth/mobile + agent-assisted** onboarding for rural reach.

## 9. Things to Avoid
- **Stock/generic imagery** — kills authenticity for a trust/impact brand ([`17`](../17-LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md)).
- **Investment/return claims** must be **honest, transparent, compliant** — funding-adjacent agri-tech has had trust crises; overpromising is an ethics/legal risk (Art. III).
- **Heavy sites for low-bandwidth users** — optimize aggressively for rural connectivity ([`35`](../35-PERFORMANCE.md), [`20`](../20-MOBILE_FIRST.md)).
- **Digital-only assumptions** — many farmers need mobile/USSD/agent-assisted, offline-tolerant flows ([`22`](../22-ACCESSIBILITY.md)).
- **Inaccessible/English-only** content excluding local-language/low-literacy users ([`22`](../22-ACCESSIBILITY.md), [`25`](../25-COPYWRITING.md)).

## 10. How to Recreate This Style Without Copying
1. Use **real, authentic photography** of your actual users/context (not stock).
2. Lead with **honest impact metrics** as trust anchors; be transparent about money/outcomes.
3. Choose a **domain-meaningful palette** (earthy/green for agriculture — your own shades).
4. Design for **low-bandwidth, mobile-first, offline-tolerant, assisted** access for underserved users.
5. Route your **two audiences** (e.g., farmer vs. funder) clearly.
6. Diverge with your own palette, photography, and voice.
